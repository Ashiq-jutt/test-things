# 3 Ocean Facts That Sound Fake
[emoji: 🌊]

# Mostly unexplored
[emoji: 🔦]
More than eighty percent of the ocean has never been seen by human eyes. We have better maps of Mars than of our own sea floor.

# The biggest waterfall
[emoji: 💧]
The tallest waterfall on Earth is underwater. Between Greenland and Iceland, cold water plunges more than three kilometers down.

# It makes our air
[emoji: 🌱]
Tiny ocean plants produce about half of the oxygen you breathe. So every second breath comes from the sea.
