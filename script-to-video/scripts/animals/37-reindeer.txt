# Meet the Reindeer
[image: photos/reindeer-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the reindeer. Reindeer live in cold, snowy places.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/reindeer-looks.jpg]
A reindeer has thick fur and big antlers. Both the mommy and the daddy have antlers.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/reindeer-where.jpg]
Reindeer live in the far north, where it is very cold.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/reindeer-eat.jpg]
Reindeer eat grass, leaves, and moss. In winter, they dig in the snow to find food.

# Made for snow
[image: photos/reindeer-extra.jpg]
Reindeer have wide feet. Their feet help them walk on top of the snow.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/reindeer-fact.jpg]
Reindeer walk a very long way every year to find food.

# See you next time
[image: photos/reindeer-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, reindeer! See you next time, friends.
