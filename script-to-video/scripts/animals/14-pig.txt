# Meet the Pig
[image: photos/pig-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the pig. Pigs are very smart animals.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/pig-looks.jpg]
A pig has a round body, a flat nose, and a curly tail. Its nose is called a snout.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/pig-where.jpg]
Pigs live on farms. Their home is called a pigsty.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/pig-eat.jpg]
Pigs eat vegetables, fruit, and grain. They use their nose to find food.

# Pigs love mud
[image: photos/pig-extra.jpg]
Pigs roll in the mud to stay cool. The mud also keeps their skin safe from the sun.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/pig-fact.jpg]
A baby pig is called a piglet. What does a pig say? Oink oink!

# See you next time
[image: photos/pig-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, pig! See you next time, friends.
