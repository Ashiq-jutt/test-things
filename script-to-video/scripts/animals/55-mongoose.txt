# Meet the Mongoose
[image: photos/mongoose-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the mongoose. The mongoose is small, quick, and brave.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/mongoose-looks.jpg]
A mongoose has a long body, short legs, and a long tail.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/mongoose-where.jpg]
Mongooses live in Africa and Asia.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/mongoose-eat.jpg]
Mongooses eat insects, eggs, lizards, and small animals.

# Brave and fast
[image: photos/mongoose-extra.jpg]
A mongoose is very fast. It can even fight a snake!

# Fun fact
[image: photos/mongoose-fact.jpg]
Some mongooses live in big family groups. They help each other look after the babies.

# See you next time
[image: photos/mongoose-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, mongoose! See you next time, friends.
