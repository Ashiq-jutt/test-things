# Meet the Kangaroo
[image: photos/kangaroo-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the kangaroo. Kangaroos love to jump.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/kangaroo-looks.jpg]
A kangaroo has big back legs, small front arms, and a long, strong tail.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/kangaroo-where.jpg]
Kangaroos live in Australia. They live in open grasslands.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/kangaroo-eat.jpg]
Kangaroos eat grass and leaves.

# The pouch
[image: photos/kangaroo-extra.jpg]
A mother kangaroo has a pouch on her tummy. Her baby rides inside the pouch.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/kangaroo-fact.jpg]
A baby kangaroo is called a joey. Can you jump like a kangaroo?

# See you next time
[image: photos/kangaroo-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, kangaroo! See you next time, friends.
