# Meet the Cat
[image: photos/cat-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the cat. Cats are soft and quiet.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/cat-looks.jpg]
A cat has soft fur, pointy ears, and long whiskers. It has sharp little claws.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/cat-where.jpg]
Many cats live at home with a family. They love warm and cozy places.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/cat-eat.jpg]
Cats eat cat food, fish, and meat.

# Sleepy cats
[image: photos/cat-extra.jpg]
Cats love to sleep. A cat sleeps for more than half of the day!

# Fun fact
[image: photos/cat-fact.jpg]
Cats can see very well when it is almost dark. And a baby cat is called a kitten. What does a cat say? Meow!

# See you next time
[image: photos/cat-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, cat! See you next time, friends.
