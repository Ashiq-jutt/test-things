# Meet the Anteater
[image: photos/anteater-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the anteater. The anteater has a very long nose.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/anteater-looks.jpg]
An anteater has a long nose, a big bushy tail, and strong claws.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/anteater-where.jpg]
Anteaters live in the forests and grasslands of Central and South America.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/anteater-eat.jpg]
Anteaters eat ants and termites. They eat thousands of them every day!

# The long tongue
[image: photos/anteater-extra.jpg]
An anteater has a very long, sticky tongue. It uses its tongue to catch ants.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/anteater-fact.jpg]
An anteater has no teeth at all! A baby anteater rides on its mother's back.

# See you next time
[image: photos/anteater-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, anteater! See you next time, friends.
