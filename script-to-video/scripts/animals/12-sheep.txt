# Meet the Sheep
[image: photos/sheep-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the sheep. Sheep are soft and woolly.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/sheep-looks.jpg]
A sheep has thick, curly wool all over its body. Most sheep are white.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/sheep-where.jpg]
Sheep live on farms. They walk on green hills and fields.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/sheep-eat.jpg]
Sheep eat grass. They eat for many hours every day.

# Sheep give us wool
[image: photos/sheep-extra.jpg]
Sheep give us wool. We use wool to make warm sweaters and blankets.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/sheep-fact.jpg]
A baby sheep is called a lamb. What does a sheep say? Baa! Can you say baa?

# See you next time
[image: photos/sheep-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, sheep! See you next time, friends.
