# Meet the Mouse
[image: photos/mouse-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the mouse. Mice are tiny and quick.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/mouse-looks.jpg]
A mouse has round ears, a pointy nose, and a long thin tail.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/mouse-where.jpg]
Mice live in fields, forests, and sometimes in houses.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/mouse-eat.jpg]
Mice eat seeds, grain, and fruit.

# Little whiskers
[image: photos/mouse-extra.jpg]
A mouse has long whiskers. The whiskers help it feel its way in the dark.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/mouse-fact.jpg]
A mouse's teeth never stop growing. What does a mouse say? Squeak squeak!

# See you next time
[image: photos/mouse-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, mouse! See you next time, friends.
