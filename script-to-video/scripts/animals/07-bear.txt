# Meet the Bear
[image: photos/bear-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the bear. Bears are big and strong.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/bear-looks.jpg]
A bear has thick fur, small round ears, and big paws with sharp claws.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/bear-where.jpg]
Many bears live in forests and mountains. Polar bears live in the cold, icy north.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/bear-eat.jpg]
Bears eat fish, berries, plants, and sweet honey.

# The long sleep
[image: photos/bear-extra.jpg]
In winter, many bears sleep for a very long time. They rest in a cozy den until spring.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/bear-fact.jpg]
A bear has a very good nose. It can smell food from far away. Can you walk like a big bear?

# See you next time
[image: photos/bear-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, bear! See you next time, friends.
