# Meet the Warthog
[image: photos/warthog-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the warthog. The warthog is a wild pig from Africa.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/warthog-looks.jpg]
A warthog has a big head, long tusks, and bumps on its face.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/warthog-where.jpg]
Warthogs live in the grasslands of Africa.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/warthog-eat.jpg]
Warthogs eat grass, roots, and fruit. They kneel down on their front knees to eat.

# A home under the ground
[image: photos/warthog-extra.jpg]
Warthogs sleep in holes under the ground. They go in backwards!

# Fun fact
[image: photos/warthog-fact.jpg]
When a warthog runs, its tail stands straight up, like a little flag!

# See you next time
[image: photos/warthog-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, warthog! See you next time, friends.
