# Meet the Jaguar
[image: photos/jaguar-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the jaguar. The jaguar is the biggest cat in the Americas.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/jaguar-looks.jpg]
A jaguar has golden fur with black spots. Its spots look like little roses.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/jaguar-where.jpg]
Jaguars live in rainforests in Central and South America.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/jaguar-eat.jpg]
Jaguars eat meat. They hunt on land and in rivers.

# Jaguars can swim
[image: photos/jaguar-extra.jpg]
Jaguars love water. They are very good swimmers.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/jaguar-fact.jpg]
A jaguar has a very strong bite. It is one of the strongest of all the big cats.

# See you next time
[image: photos/jaguar-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, jaguar! See you next time, friends.
