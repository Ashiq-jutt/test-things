# Meet the Donkey
[image: photos/donkey-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the donkey. Donkeys are strong and gentle.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/donkey-looks.jpg]
A donkey looks like a small horse. It has very long ears.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/donkey-where.jpg]
Donkeys live on farms. They can also live in hot, dry places.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/donkey-eat.jpg]
Donkeys eat grass, hay, and straw.

# Donkeys help people
[image: photos/donkey-extra.jpg]
Donkeys are very strong. They carry heavy things for people.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/donkey-fact.jpg]
A donkey's call is very loud. What does a donkey say? Hee-haw!
