# Meet the Bat
[image: photos/bat-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the bat. A bat is not a bird, but it can fly!

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/bat-looks.jpg]
A bat has a furry body, big ears, and wings made of thin skin.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/bat-where.jpg]
Bats live in caves, trees, and old buildings.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/bat-eat.jpg]
Many bats eat insects. Some bats eat fruit.

# Upside down
[image: photos/bat-extra.jpg]
Bats sleep in the day. They hang upside down by their feet!

# Fun fact
[image: photos/bat-fact.jpg]
Bats fly at night. Many bats use sound to find their way in the dark.

# See you next time
[image: photos/bat-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, bat! See you next time, friends.
