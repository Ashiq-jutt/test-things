# Meet the Alpaca
[image: photos/alpaca-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the alpaca. Alpacas are small and very fluffy.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/alpaca-looks.jpg]
An alpaca looks like a small llama. It has soft, fluffy wool and a short face.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/alpaca-where.jpg]
Alpacas live in the mountains of South America. Many alpacas live on farms.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/alpaca-eat.jpg]
Alpacas eat grass and hay.

# Soft wool
[image: photos/alpaca-extra.jpg]
Alpaca wool is very soft and warm. People use it to make sweaters and scarves.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/alpaca-fact.jpg]
Alpacas come in many colors, like white, brown, and black. They make a soft humming sound.

# See you next time
[image: photos/alpaca-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, alpaca! See you next time, friends.
