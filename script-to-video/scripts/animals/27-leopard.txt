# Meet the Leopard
[image: photos/leopard-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the leopard. Leopards are strong cats with spots.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/leopard-looks.jpg]
A leopard has golden fur with dark spots. It has a long tail.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/leopard-where.jpg]
Leopards live in Africa and Asia. They live in forests and grasslands.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/leopard-eat.jpg]
Leopards eat meat. They hunt mostly at night.

# Tree climbers
[image: photos/leopard-extra.jpg]
Leopards are great climbers. They rest on tree branches. They even carry their food up the tree.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/leopard-fact.jpg]
A leopard's spots help it hide in the grass and trees.

# See you next time
[image: photos/leopard-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, leopard! See you next time, friends.
