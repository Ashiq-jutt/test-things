# Meet the Tortoise
[image: photos/tortoise-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the tortoise. The tortoise is slow and steady.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/tortoise-looks.jpg]
A tortoise has a hard round shell, short strong legs, and wrinkly skin.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/tortoise-where.jpg]
Tortoises live on land. They live in warm, dry places.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/tortoise-eat.jpg]
Tortoises eat grass, leaves, flowers, and fruit.

# A house on its back
[image: photos/tortoise-extra.jpg]
The shell is the home of the tortoise. When it is scared, it pulls its head and legs inside.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/tortoise-fact.jpg]
Tortoises live for a very long time. Some tortoises live for more than one hundred years!

# See you next time
[image: photos/tortoise-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, tortoise! See you next time, friends.
