# Meet the Bison
[image: photos/bison-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the bison. The bison is the biggest land animal in North America.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/bison-looks.jpg]
A bison has a huge head, a hump on its back, and thick brown fur.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/bison-where.jpg]
Bison live on the wide grasslands of North America.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/bison-eat.jpg]
Bison eat grass.

# Warm in winter
[image: photos/bison-extra.jpg]
A bison has a thick coat. It keeps the bison warm in the cold snow.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/bison-fact.jpg]
A bison is very big, but it can run very fast!

# See you next time
[image: photos/bison-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, bison! See you next time, friends.
