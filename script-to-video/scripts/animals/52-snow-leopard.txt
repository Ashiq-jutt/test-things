# Meet the Snow Leopard
[image: photos/snow-leopard-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the snow leopard. The snow leopard lives high in the snowy mountains.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/snow-leopard-looks.jpg]
A snow leopard has thick gray fur with dark spots. It has a very long, fluffy tail.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/snow-leopard-where.jpg]
Snow leopards live in the cold mountains of Asia.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/snow-leopard-eat.jpg]
Snow leopards eat meat. They hunt wild sheep and goats.

# Made for snow
[image: photos/snow-leopard-extra.jpg]
A snow leopard has big, furry paws. They work like snowshoes on the snow.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/snow-leopard-fact.jpg]
A snow leopard cannot roar. But it can jump very, very far!

# See you next time
[image: photos/snow-leopard-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, snow leopard! See you next time, friends.
