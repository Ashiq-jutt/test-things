# Meet the Deer
[image: photos/deer-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the deer. Deer are gentle and shy.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/deer-looks.jpg]
A deer has a brown coat and long, thin legs. The daddy deer has antlers on his head.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/deer-where.jpg]
Deer live in forests and in fields.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/deer-eat.jpg]
Deer eat grass, leaves, and berries.

# Antlers
[image: photos/deer-extra.jpg]
Antlers fall off every year. Then new antlers grow again!

# Fun fact
[image: photos/deer-fact.jpg]
A baby deer is called a fawn. It has white spots on its back.

# See you next time
[image: photos/deer-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, deer! See you next time, friends.
