# Meet the Chimpanzee
[image: photos/chimpanzee-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the chimpanzee. Chimpanzees are very clever.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/chimpanzee-looks.jpg]
A chimpanzee has black hair, long arms, and big ears. It has no tail.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/chimpanzee-where.jpg]
Chimpanzees live in the forests of Africa.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/chimpanzee-eat.jpg]
Chimpanzees eat fruit, leaves, nuts, and insects.

# Using tools
[image: photos/chimpanzee-extra.jpg]
Chimpanzees use tools. They use a stick to catch insects and a stone to open nuts.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/chimpanzee-fact.jpg]
Chimpanzees hug and hold hands, just like people do.

# See you next time
[image: photos/chimpanzee-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, chimpanzee! See you next time, friends.
