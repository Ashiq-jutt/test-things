# Meet the Monkey
[image: photos/monkey-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the monkey. Monkeys are clever and playful.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/monkey-looks.jpg]
A monkey has long arms, long legs, and a long tail. Its hands look a little like your hands.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/monkey-where.jpg]
Many monkeys live in forests. They love to climb and jump from tree to tree.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/monkey-eat.jpg]
Monkeys eat fruit, leaves, nuts, and small insects.

# Monkey family
[image: photos/monkey-extra.jpg]
Monkeys live together in a group. A group of monkeys is called a troop.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/monkey-fact.jpg]
Many monkeys use their tail to keep their balance. Can you jump like a monkey?

# See you next time
[image: photos/monkey-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, monkey! See you next time, friends.
