# What does it look like?
[image: photos/gorilla-looks.jpg]
A gorilla has black fur, long arms, and a big chest.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/gorilla-where.jpg]
Gorillas live in the forests of Africa.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/gorilla-eat.jpg]
Gorillas eat leaves, stems, and fruit.

[image: photos/gorilla-extra.jpg]
Gorillas live in a family group. The leader is a big daddy gorilla called a silverback.
