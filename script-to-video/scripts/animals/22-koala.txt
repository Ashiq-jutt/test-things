# Meet the Koala
[image: photos/koala-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the koala. Koalas are soft and sleepy.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/koala-looks.jpg]
A koala has gray fur, round fluffy ears, and a big black nose.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/koala-where.jpg]
Koalas live in Australia. They live high up in the trees.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/koala-eat.jpg]
Koalas eat leaves from the eucalyptus tree.

# Sleepy koalas
[image: photos/koala-extra.jpg]
Koalas sleep for most of the day. They hold on to the tree while they sleep.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/koala-fact.jpg]
A koala is not a bear. A mother koala carries her baby in a pouch, just like a kangaroo.

# See you next time
[image: photos/koala-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, koala! See you next time, friends.
