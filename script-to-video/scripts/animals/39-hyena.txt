# Meet the Hyena
[image: photos/hyena-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the hyena. Hyenas are famous for their funny laugh.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/hyena-looks.jpg]
A hyena has spotted fur, round ears, and a strong body. Its front legs are longer than its back legs.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/hyena-where.jpg]
Hyenas live in the grasslands of Africa.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/hyena-eat.jpg]
Hyenas eat meat. They have very strong teeth and can even chew bones.

# Hyena family
[image: photos/hyena-extra.jpg]
Hyenas live in a big group called a clan. The leader of the clan is a female.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/hyena-fact.jpg]
A hyena makes a sound like a laugh. Can you laugh like a hyena?

# See you next time
[image: photos/hyena-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, hyena! See you next time, friends.
