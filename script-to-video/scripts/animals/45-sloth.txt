# Meet the Sloth
[image: photos/sloth-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the sloth. The sloth is a very, very slow animal.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/sloth-looks.jpg]
A sloth has long arms and long curved claws. Its face looks like it is smiling.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/sloth-where.jpg]
Sloths live in rainforests in Central and South America. They live up in the trees.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/sloth-eat.jpg]
Sloths eat leaves, buds, and fruit.

# Upside down
[image: photos/sloth-extra.jpg]
Sloths hang upside down from branches. They even sleep upside down!

# Fun fact
[image: photos/sloth-fact.jpg]
Sloths move very slowly on land. But they are good swimmers!

# See you next time
[image: photos/sloth-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, sloth! See you next time, friends.
