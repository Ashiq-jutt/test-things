# Meet the Camel
[image: photos/camel-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the camel. People call the camel the ship of the desert.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/camel-looks.jpg]
A camel has long legs, a long neck, and a hump on its back. Some camels have two humps.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/camel-where.jpg]
Camels live in the desert. The desert is hot, dry, and full of sand.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/camel-eat.jpg]
Camels eat grass, leaves, and dry desert plants.

# The hump
[image: photos/camel-extra.jpg]
A camel keeps fat in its hump. This helps the camel when there is no food.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/camel-fact.jpg]
A camel can go many days without drinking water. It has long eyelashes to keep sand out of its eyes.

# See you next time
[image: photos/camel-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, camel! See you next time, friends.
