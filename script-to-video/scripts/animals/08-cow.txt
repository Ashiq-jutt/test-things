# Meet the Cow
[image: photos/cow-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the cow. The cow is a gentle farm animal.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/cow-looks.jpg]
A cow has a big body, four legs, and a long tail. Cows can be black, white, or brown.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/cow-where.jpg]
Cows live on a farm. They like to walk in green fields.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/cow-eat.jpg]
Cows eat grass and hay. They chew their food again and again.

# Cows give us milk
[image: photos/cow-extra.jpg]
Cows give us milk. We use milk to make butter, cheese, and yogurt.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/cow-fact.jpg]
A baby cow is called a calf. And what does a cow say? Moo! Can you say moo?

# See you next time
[image: photos/cow-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, cow! See you next time, friends.
