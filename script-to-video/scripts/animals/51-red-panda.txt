# Meet the Red Panda
[image: photos/red-panda-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the red panda. The red panda is small and fluffy.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/red-panda-looks.jpg]
A red panda has red fur, a white face, and a long tail with rings.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/red-panda-where.jpg]
Red pandas live in mountain forests in Asia. They live up in the trees.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/red-panda-eat.jpg]
Red pandas eat bamboo, fruit, and eggs.

# A warm tail
[image: photos/red-panda-extra.jpg]
A red panda wraps its fluffy tail around its body. It works like a warm blanket.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/red-panda-fact.jpg]
A red panda is about as big as a house cat. It sleeps in the day and wakes up in the evening.

# See you next time
[image: photos/red-panda-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, red panda! See you next time, friends.
