# Meet the Hedgehog
[image: photos/hedgehog-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the hedgehog. Hedgehogs are small and spiky.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/hedgehog-looks.jpg]
A hedgehog has sharp spikes on its back. It has a small face and a pointy nose.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/hedgehog-where.jpg]
Hedgehogs live in gardens, fields, and forests.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/hedgehog-eat.jpg]
Hedgehogs eat insects, worms, and snails.

# Rolling into a ball
[image: photos/hedgehog-extra.jpg]
When a hedgehog is scared, it rolls into a ball. The spikes keep it safe.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/hedgehog-fact.jpg]
Hedgehogs sleep in the day and wake up at night.

# See you next time
[image: photos/hedgehog-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, hedgehog! See you next time, friends.
