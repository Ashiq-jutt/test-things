# Meet the Goat
[image: photos/goat-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the goat. Goats are clever and love to climb.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/goat-looks.jpg]
A goat has two horns and a little beard under its chin.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/goat-where.jpg]
Goats live on farms. Wild goats live high up in the mountains.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/goat-eat.jpg]
Goats eat grass, leaves, and small plants.

# Great climbers
[image: photos/goat-extra.jpg]
Goats are very good at climbing. They can walk on steep rocks and they do not fall.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/goat-fact.jpg]
A baby goat is called a kid. Yes, just like you! What does a goat say? Maa!

# See you next time
[image: photos/goat-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, goat! See you next time, friends.
