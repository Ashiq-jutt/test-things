# Meet the Giraffe
[emoji: 🦒]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the giraffe. It is the tallest animal in the world.

# What does it look like?
[emoji: 👀]
A giraffe has a very long neck and very long legs. Its body has brown spots.

# Where does it live?
[emoji: 🌍]
Giraffes live in Africa. They walk in open grasslands with tall trees.

# What does it eat?
[emoji: 🌿]
Giraffes eat leaves from the top of tall trees. No other animal can reach so high!

# The long tongue
[emoji: 👅]
A giraffe has a long, dark tongue. It is about as long as your arm!

# Fun fact
[emoji: ⭐]
A baby giraffe can stand up and walk soon after it is born. Can you stretch up tall like a giraffe?

# See you next time
[emoji: 👋]
Goodbye, giraffe! See you next time, friends.
