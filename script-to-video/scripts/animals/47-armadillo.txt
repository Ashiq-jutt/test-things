# Meet the Armadillo
[image: photos/armadillo-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the armadillo. The armadillo wears a suit of armor.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/armadillo-looks.jpg]
An armadillo has a hard shell on its back, a pointy nose, and strong claws.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/armadillo-where.jpg]
Armadillos live in warm places in North and South America.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/armadillo-eat.jpg]
Armadillos eat insects, worms, and plants.

# A hard shell
[image: photos/armadillo-extra.jpg]
The hard shell keeps the armadillo safe. Some armadillos can roll into a ball!

# Fun fact
[image: photos/armadillo-fact.jpg]
Armadillos are great diggers. They sleep for most of the day.

# See you next time
[image: photos/armadillo-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, armadillo! See you next time, friends.
