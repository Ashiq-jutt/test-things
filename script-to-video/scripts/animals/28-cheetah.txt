# Meet the Cheetah
[image: photos/cheetah-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the cheetah. The cheetah is the fastest animal on land.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/cheetah-looks.jpg]
A cheetah has a thin body, long legs, and black spots. It has black lines on its face that look like tears.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/cheetah-where.jpg]
Cheetahs live in the grasslands of Africa.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/cheetah-eat.jpg]
Cheetahs eat meat. They hunt in the daytime.

# So fast!
[image: photos/cheetah-extra.jpg]
A cheetah can run as fast as a car on the road. But it gets tired quickly.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/cheetah-fact.jpg]
Cheetahs cannot roar like lions. They purr like a cat instead!

# See you next time
[image: photos/cheetah-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, cheetah! See you next time, friends.
