# Meet the Chameleon
[image: photos/chameleon-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the chameleon. The chameleon is a lizard that can change color.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/chameleon-looks.jpg]
A chameleon has bumpy skin, a curly tail, and feet that hold on to branches.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/chameleon-where.jpg]
Many chameleons live in Africa and Madagascar. They live in trees and bushes.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/chameleon-eat.jpg]
Chameleons eat insects.

# The fast tongue
[image: photos/chameleon-extra.jpg]
A chameleon has a very long, sticky tongue. It shoots out to catch insects in a flash!

# Fun fact
[image: photos/chameleon-fact.jpg]
A chameleon can move its two eyes in different ways. One eye looks forward and one eye looks back!

# See you next time
[image: photos/chameleon-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, chameleon! See you next time, friends.
