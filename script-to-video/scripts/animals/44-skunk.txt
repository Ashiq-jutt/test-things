# Meet the Skunk
[image: photos/skunk-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the skunk. Skunks are black and white.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/skunk-looks.jpg]
A skunk has black fur with white stripes and a big fluffy tail.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/skunk-where.jpg]
Skunks live in forests and fields in North and South America.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/skunk-eat.jpg]
Skunks eat insects, worms, fruit, and plants.

# A stinky spray
[image: photos/skunk-extra.jpg]
When a skunk is scared, it sprays a very stinky smell. Then other animals run away!

# Fun fact
[image: photos/skunk-fact.jpg]
Before it sprays, a skunk stamps its feet. That means, stay away!

# See you next time
[image: photos/skunk-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, skunk! See you next time, friends.
