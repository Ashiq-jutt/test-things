# Meet the Hamster
[image: photos/hamster-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the hamster. Hamsters are tiny and cute.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/hamster-looks.jpg]
A hamster has soft fur, small round ears, and a very short tail.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/hamster-where.jpg]
Many hamsters live at home as pets. Wild hamsters live in dry places and dig homes under the ground.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/hamster-eat.jpg]
Hamsters eat seeds, grain, and vegetables.

# Big cheeks
[image: photos/hamster-extra.jpg]
A hamster has big cheeks. It fills them with food and carries the food home.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/hamster-fact.jpg]
Hamsters sleep in the day and play at night. They love to run on a wheel!

# See you next time
[image: photos/hamster-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, hamster! See you next time, friends.
