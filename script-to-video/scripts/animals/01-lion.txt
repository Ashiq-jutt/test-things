# Meet the Lion
[emoji: 🦁]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the lion. People call the lion the king of the jungle.

# What does it look like?
[emoji: 👀]
A lion is a very big cat with golden fur. The daddy lion has thick hair around his head. It is called a mane.

# Where does it live?
[emoji: 🌍]
Lions live in the grasslands of Africa. A small number of lions also live in India.

# What does it eat?
[emoji: 🍖]
Lions eat meat. The mother lions do most of the hunting.

# Lion family
[emoji: 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦]
Lions live together in a big family. A lion family is called a pride.

# Fun fact
[emoji: ⭐]
A lion's roar is very, very loud. You can hear it from far away. Can you roar like a lion? Roar!

# See you next time
[emoji: 👋]
Goodbye, lion! See you next time, friends.
