# Meet the Panda
[image: photos/panda-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the panda. Pandas are black and white bears.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/panda-looks.jpg]
A panda has white fur with black ears, black legs, and black patches around its eyes.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/panda-where.jpg]
Pandas live in China. They live in cool mountain forests.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/panda-eat.jpg]
Pandas eat bamboo. They eat for many hours every day.

# Playful pandas
[image: photos/panda-extra.jpg]
Pandas love to climb trees and roll on the ground.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/panda-fact.jpg]
A baby panda is very tiny when it is born. It is pink and has almost no fur!

# See you next time
[image: photos/panda-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, panda! See you next time, friends.
