# Meet the Moose
[image: photos/moose-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the moose. The moose is the biggest deer in the world.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/moose-looks.jpg]
A moose has long legs, a big nose, and huge flat antlers.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/moose-where.jpg]
Moose live in cold forests in the north. They live near lakes and rivers.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/moose-eat.jpg]
Moose eat leaves, twigs, and water plants.

# Huge antlers
[image: photos/moose-extra.jpg]
Only the daddy moose has antlers. They fall off in winter and grow back in spring.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/moose-fact.jpg]
A moose is a good swimmer. It can put its head under the water to eat plants.

# See you next time
[image: photos/moose-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, moose! See you next time, friends.
