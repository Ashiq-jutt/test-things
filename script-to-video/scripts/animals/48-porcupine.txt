# Meet the Porcupine
[image: photos/porcupine-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the porcupine. Porcupines are covered with sharp quills.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/porcupine-looks.jpg]
A porcupine has thousands of long, sharp quills on its back and tail.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/porcupine-where.jpg]
Porcupines live in forests, deserts, and grasslands.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/porcupine-eat.jpg]
Porcupines eat leaves, fruit, and bark from trees.

# Sharp quills
[image: photos/porcupine-extra.jpg]
When a porcupine is scared, it lifts its quills. Then other animals stay away.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/porcupine-fact.jpg]
A baby porcupine has soft quills when it is born. They soon become hard.

# See you next time
[image: photos/porcupine-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, porcupine! See you next time, friends.
