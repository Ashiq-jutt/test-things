# Meet the Fox
[image: photos/fox-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the fox. Foxes are clever and quick.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/fox-looks.jpg]
A fox has orange fur, pointy ears, and a big fluffy tail.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/fox-where.jpg]
Foxes live in forests and fields. Some foxes even live near towns.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/fox-eat.jpg]
Foxes eat mice, birds, insects, and fruit.

# A cozy home
[image: photos/fox-extra.jpg]
A fox lives in a home under the ground. It is called a den.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/fox-fact.jpg]
A fox can hear a mouse moving under the snow! A baby fox is called a kit.

# See you next time
[image: photos/fox-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, fox! See you next time, friends.
