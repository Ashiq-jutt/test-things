# Meet the Orangutan
[image: photos/orangutan-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the orangutan. Orangutans have long orange hair.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/orangutan-looks.jpg]
An orangutan has orange hair and very long arms. Its arms are longer than its legs.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/orangutan-where.jpg]
Orangutans live in rainforests in Asia. They spend most of their time up in the trees.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/orangutan-eat.jpg]
Orangutans eat fruit and leaves.

# Swinging in the trees
[image: photos/orangutan-extra.jpg]
Orangutans swing from branch to branch with their long arms.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/orangutan-fact.jpg]
An orangutan makes a bed of leaves in the trees every night. When it rains, it holds a big leaf like an umbrella!

# See you next time
[image: photos/orangutan-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, orangutan! See you next time, friends.
