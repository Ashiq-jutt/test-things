# Meet the Horse
[image: photos/horse-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the horse. Horses are fast and strong.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/horse-looks.jpg]
A horse has four long legs and a long tail. It has long hair on its neck. It is called a mane.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/horse-where.jpg]
Horses live on farms and in open fields. Some horses still live in the wild.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/horse-eat.jpg]
Horses eat grass and hay. They also love apples and carrots.

# Horses help people
[image: photos/horse-extra.jpg]
People ride horses. Horses can also pull carts and help on farms.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/horse-fact.jpg]
A horse can sleep standing up! And a baby horse is called a foal. What does a horse say? Neigh!

# See you next time
[image: photos/horse-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, horse! See you next time, friends.
