# Meet the Lynx
[image: photos/lynx-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the lynx. The lynx is a wild cat with pointy ears.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/lynx-looks.jpg]
A lynx has thick fur, a short tail, and black tufts of hair on top of its ears.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/lynx-where.jpg]
Lynx live in cold forests and mountains.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/lynx-eat.jpg]
Lynx eat meat. They hunt rabbits and birds.

# Big paws
[image: photos/lynx-extra.jpg]
A lynx has big, furry paws. They help it walk on deep snow.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/lynx-fact.jpg]
A lynx can see and hear very well. It hunts quietly at night.

# See you next time
[image: photos/lynx-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, lynx! See you next time, friends.
