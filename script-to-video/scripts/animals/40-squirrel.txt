# Meet the Squirrel
[image: photos/squirrel-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the squirrel. Squirrels are small and quick.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/squirrel-looks.jpg]
A squirrel has soft fur, bright eyes, and a big bushy tail.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/squirrel-where.jpg]
Squirrels live in trees. You can see them in forests and in parks.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/squirrel-eat.jpg]
Squirrels eat nuts, seeds, and fruit.

# Saving food
[image: photos/squirrel-extra.jpg]
Squirrels hide nuts in the ground. They save them to eat in winter.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/squirrel-fact.jpg]
A squirrel uses its tail to keep its balance. When it is cold, the tail is a warm blanket.

# See you next time
[image: photos/squirrel-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, squirrel! See you next time, friends.
