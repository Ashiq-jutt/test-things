# Meet the Rhinoceros
[image: photos/rhinoceros-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the rhinoceros. We can also call it a rhino.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/rhinoceros-looks.jpg]
A rhino has a big body, thick gray skin, and a horn on its nose.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/rhinoceros-where.jpg]
Rhinos live in Africa and Asia. They live in grasslands and forests.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/rhinoceros-eat.jpg]
Rhinos eat grass, leaves, and fruit.

# Mud bath
[image: photos/rhinoceros-extra.jpg]
Rhinos love to roll in the mud. The mud keeps their skin cool and safe from the sun.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/rhinoceros-fact.jpg]
A rhino's horn is made of the same thing as your fingernails!

# See you next time
[image: photos/rhinoceros-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, rhinoceros! See you next time, friends.
