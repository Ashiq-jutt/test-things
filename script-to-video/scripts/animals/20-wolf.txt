# Meet the Wolf
[image: photos/wolf-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the wolf. Wolves look like big wild dogs.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/wolf-looks.jpg]
A wolf has thick fur, pointy ears, and a bushy tail.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/wolf-where.jpg]
Wolves live in forests, mountains, and cold snowy places.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/wolf-eat.jpg]
Wolves eat meat. They hunt together as a team.

# Wolf family
[image: photos/wolf-extra.jpg]
Wolves live together in a family. A wolf family is called a pack.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/wolf-fact.jpg]
Wolves howl to talk to each other. Can you howl like a wolf? Awoo!

# See you next time
[image: photos/wolf-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, wolf! See you next time, friends.
