# Meet the Raccoon
[image: photos/raccoon-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the raccoon. Raccoons look like they are wearing a mask.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/raccoon-looks.jpg]
A raccoon has gray fur, a black mask around its eyes, and a tail with rings.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/raccoon-where.jpg]
Raccoons live in forests in North America. Some raccoons live near towns.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/raccoon-eat.jpg]
Raccoons eat fruit, nuts, insects, and eggs.

# Clever hands
[image: photos/raccoon-extra.jpg]
Raccoons have clever little hands. They can open doors, jars, and boxes!

# Fun fact
[image: photos/raccoon-fact.jpg]
Raccoons are awake at night. They are very good climbers.

Goodbye, raccoon! See you next time, friends.
