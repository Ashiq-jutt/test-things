# Meet the Yak
[image: photos/yak-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the yak. The yak looks like a cow with very long hair.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/yak-looks.jpg]
A yak has long, thick hair and big horns. Its hair almost touches the ground.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/yak-where.jpg]
Yaks live high up in the cold mountains of Asia.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/yak-eat.jpg]
Yaks eat grass and small mountain plants.

# Yaks help people
[image: photos/yak-extra.jpg]
Yaks carry heavy things up the mountains. They also give milk and wool.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/yak-fact.jpg]
A yak's thick coat keeps it warm, even in the freezing snow.

# See you next time
[image: photos/yak-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, yak! See you next time, friends.
