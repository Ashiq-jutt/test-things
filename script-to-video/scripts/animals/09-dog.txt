# Meet the Dog
[emoji: 🐶]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the dog. Dogs are our best friends.

# What does it look like?
[emoji: 👀]
A dog has four legs, a wet nose, and a wagging tail. Dogs can be big or small.

# Where does it live?
[emoji: 🏡]
Many dogs live at home with a family. They love to play and go for walks.

# What does it eat?
[emoji: 🦴]
Dogs eat dog food and meat. They need fresh water every day.

# Dogs help people
[emoji: 🦮]
Dogs are very helpful. Some dogs guard our homes. Some dogs help people who cannot see.

# Fun fact
[emoji: ⭐]
A dog can smell much better than you can. And a baby dog is called a puppy. What does a dog say? Woof woof!

# See you next time
[emoji: 👋]
Goodbye, dog! See you next time, friends.
