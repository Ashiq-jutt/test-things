# Meet the Zebra
[image: photos/zebra-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the zebra. A zebra looks like a horse with stripes.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/zebra-looks.jpg]
A zebra has black and white stripes all over its body. It has a short mane that stands up.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/zebra-where.jpg]
Zebras live in Africa. They live in wide, open grasslands.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/zebra-eat.jpg]
Zebras eat grass. They spend most of the day eating.

# Zebra family
[image: photos/zebra-extra.jpg]
Zebras live together in a group. A group of zebras is called a herd. They stay close to keep safe.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/zebra-fact.jpg]
Every zebra has its own stripes. No two zebras look the same!

# See you next time
[image: photos/zebra-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, zebra! See you next time, friends.
