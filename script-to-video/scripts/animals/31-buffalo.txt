# Meet the Buffalo
[image: photos/buffalo-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the buffalo. Buffaloes are big and strong.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/buffalo-looks.jpg]
A buffalo has a big dark body and two large curved horns.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/buffalo-where.jpg]
Some buffaloes live in the grasslands of Africa. Others live on farms in Asia.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/buffalo-eat.jpg]
Buffaloes eat grass and plants.

# Buffalo herd
[image: photos/buffalo-extra.jpg]
Buffaloes live in a big group called a herd. They look after each other.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/buffalo-fact.jpg]
On many farms, buffaloes give milk, just like cows.

# See you next time
[image: photos/buffalo-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, buffalo! See you next time, friends.
