# Meet the Rabbit
[image: photos/rabbit-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the rabbit. Rabbits are soft and quick.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/rabbit-looks.jpg]
A rabbit has long ears, soft fur, and a small fluffy tail.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/rabbit-where.jpg]
Wild rabbits live in fields and forests. They dig their homes under the ground.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/rabbit-eat.jpg]
Rabbits eat grass, leaves, and vegetables.

# Hop, hop, hop
[image: photos/rabbit-extra.jpg]
Rabbits have strong back legs. They hop and jump very fast.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/rabbit-fact.jpg]
A rabbit's teeth never stop growing. Can you hop like a rabbit?

# See you next time
[image: photos/rabbit-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, rabbit! See you next time, friends.
