# Meet the Tiger
[emoji: 🐯]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the tiger. The tiger is the biggest cat in the world.

# What does it look like?
[emoji: 👀]
A tiger has orange fur with black stripes. It has sharp teeth and a long tail.

# Where does it live?
[emoji: 🌍]
Tigers live in Asia. They live in forests and in tall grass.

# What does it eat?
[emoji: 🍖]
Tigers eat meat. They hunt quietly, mostly at night.

# Tigers love water
[emoji: 🏊]
Most cats do not like water. But tigers love to swim! They jump in the water to cool down.

# Fun fact
[emoji: ⭐]
No two tigers have the same stripes. Every tiger is special, just like you!

# See you next time
[emoji: 👋]
Goodbye, tiger! See you next time, friends.
