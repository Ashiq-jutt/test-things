# Meet the Elephant
[emoji: 🐘]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the elephant. It is the biggest animal that walks on land.

# What does it look like?
[emoji: 👀]
An elephant has a long nose called a trunk. It has two big ears and thick gray skin.

# Where does it live?
[emoji: 🌍]
Elephants live in Africa and Asia. They live in forests and in grasslands.

# What does it eat?
[emoji: 🌿]
Elephants eat grass, leaves, and fruit. They eat for many hours every day.

# The amazing trunk
[emoji: 💦]
An elephant uses its trunk to smell, to drink water, and to pick up food. It can even spray water like a shower!

# Fun fact
[emoji: ⭐]
Elephants flap their big ears to stay cool. Can you flap your ears like an elephant?

# See you next time
[emoji: 👋]
Goodbye, elephant! See you next time, friends.
