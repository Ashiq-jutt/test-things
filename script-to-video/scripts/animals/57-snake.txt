# Meet the Snake
[image: photos/snake-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the snake. Snakes have no arms and no legs.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/snake-looks.jpg]
A snake has a long, thin body covered with scales.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/snake-where.jpg]
Snakes live in forests, deserts, and grasslands.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/snake-eat.jpg]
Snakes eat mice, frogs, eggs, and insects. They swallow their food whole.

# How snakes move
[image: photos/snake-extra.jpg]
A snake slides on its tummy. It moves its body from side to side.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/snake-fact.jpg]
A snake smells with its tongue! And when a snake grows, it takes off its old skin.

# See you next time
[image: photos/snake-bye.jpg]
Remember, never touch a snake outside. Goodbye, snake! See you next time, friends.
