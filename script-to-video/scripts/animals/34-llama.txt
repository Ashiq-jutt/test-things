# Meet the Llama
[image: photos/llama-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the llama. Llamas are friendly animals with long necks.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/llama-looks.jpg]
A llama has a long neck, long legs, and thick woolly fur. Its ears are shaped like bananas.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/llama-where.jpg]
Llamas live in the mountains of South America.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/llama-eat.jpg]
Llamas eat grass and plants.

# Llamas help people
[image: photos/llama-extra.jpg]
Llamas carry bags for people on mountain paths.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/llama-fact.jpg]
When a llama is angry, it spits! Llamas also hum to talk to each other.

# See you next time
[image: photos/llama-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, llama! See you next time, friends.
