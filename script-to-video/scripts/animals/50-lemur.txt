# Meet the Lemur
[image: photos/lemur-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the lemur. Lemurs have big bright eyes.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/lemur-looks.jpg]
A lemur has soft fur, big eyes, and a long tail. Some lemurs have black and white rings on their tail.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/lemur-where.jpg]
Lemurs live on an island called Madagascar. They live nowhere else in the wild.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/lemur-eat.jpg]
Lemurs eat fruit, leaves, and flowers.

# Sunbathing
[image: photos/lemur-extra.jpg]
Lemurs love the sun. They sit up and open their arms to get warm.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/lemur-fact.jpg]
Lemurs live in a group. In a lemur group, the females are the leaders.

# See you next time
[image: photos/lemur-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, lemur! See you next time, friends.
