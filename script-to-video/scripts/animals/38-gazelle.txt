# Meet the Gazelle
[image: photos/gazelle-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the gazelle. Gazelles are fast and graceful.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/gazelle-looks.jpg]
A gazelle has a light brown body, a white tummy, and long thin legs. It has two horns.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/gazelle-where.jpg]
Gazelles live in the grasslands and deserts of Africa and Asia.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/gazelle-eat.jpg]
Gazelles eat grass, leaves, and small plants.

# Run and jump
[image: photos/gazelle-extra.jpg]
Gazelles run very fast. They also jump high into the air with all four feet!

# Fun fact
[image: photos/gazelle-fact.jpg]
Gazelles live in a herd. Many eyes help to watch for danger.

# See you next time
[image: photos/gazelle-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, gazelle! See you next time, friends.
