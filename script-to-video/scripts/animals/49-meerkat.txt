# Meet the Meerkat
[image: photos/meerkat-title.jpg]
Hello friends! Today we will meet the meerkat. Meerkats are small and very curious.

# What does it look like?
[image: photos/meerkat-looks.jpg]
A meerkat has a thin body, a pointy face, and dark patches around its eyes.

# Where does it live?
[image: photos/meerkat-where.jpg]
Meerkats live in the deserts of southern Africa. Their homes are under the ground.

# What does it eat?
[image: photos/meerkat-eat.jpg]
Meerkats eat insects, lizards, and small animals.

# Standing guard
[image: photos/meerkat-extra.jpg]
One meerkat stands up tall and looks for danger. It calls out to keep the family safe.

# Fun fact
[image: photos/meerkat-fact.jpg]
Meerkats live in a big group called a mob. Can you stand up tall like a meerkat?

# See you next time
[image: photos/meerkat-bye.jpg]
Goodbye, meerkat! See you next time, friends.
